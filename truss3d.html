1. 3D Truss Model

A 3D truss member carries axial force only. Each node has three translational DOFs: u, v and w in the global X, Y and Z directions.

Direction cosines: l = Δx/L, m = Δy/L, n = Δz/L. The member direction is Node i → Node j.
